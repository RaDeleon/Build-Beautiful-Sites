# Vertical Recipes

Use these as story structures, not copy-paste brands. Replace every claim, price, metric, product detail, schedule, location, and proof point with verified client information.

## Premium physical product

- Story: reveal → macro material detail → construction/exploded logic → scarcity/proof → inquiry or purchase.
- Media: one approved master product still; slow orbit; macro glide; assembly/transformation.
- Interface: scrubbed hero, restrained specification callouts, edition/availability, qualified CTA.

## Experience or destination

- Story: threshold → progressive journey → peak moment → practical details → booking.
- Media: one environment/vehicle reference; continuous descent, ascent, fly-through, or route with chained endpoints.
- Interface: progress/depth/altitude indicator tied to real story chapters, not decorative numbers.

## Personal portfolio

- Story: positioning → capabilities → selected proof → working style → contact.
- Media: a consented, high-quality identity reference; one controlled hero take; real work imagery rather than invented holographic screens when available.
- Interface: case studies before vanity counters; only verified numbers; direct inquiry CTA.

## E-commerce release

- Story: campaign reveal → product family → materials/details → fit/use → purchase assurance.
- Media: actual product references; consistent garment/product geometry; short per-product turntables where valuable.
- Interface: functional product selection, cart, inventory, shipping/returns, and checkout for production. Label demo drawers as prototypes.

## Restaurant or hospitality

- Story: signature sensory moment → room/experience → menu or offering → social proof → reservation.
- Media: actual venue and food should replace generated imagery for production; use AI for concept direction or missing transitional shots only with approval.
- Interface: readable menu, current hours, location, accessibility information, and a real booking path.

## Property or real estate

- Story: exterior context → arrival → flow through key rooms → defining view/amenity → private showing.
- Media: actual listing photography as reference; do not generate architectural features that do not exist.
- Interface: verified facts, disclosures/agent details, gallery fallback, inquiry routing, and truthful availability.

## Vehicle or machine

- Story: reveal → motion/use → engineering proof → environment/night state → reservation or inquiry.
- Media: one approved master product render; controlled tracking; macro engineering details; consistency prioritized over spectacle.
- Interface: verified performance data, accessible alternatives to speed HUDs, and a real configurator only when product data supports it.

## SaaS or application

- Story: painful signal → product intervention → workflow → measurable outcome → trial/demo.
- Media: abstract cinematic hero may set tone, but real product screenshots or recordings must prove the product.
- Interface: truthful metrics, product UI, integrations/security, pricing when known, FAQ, and working signup/demo flow.

## Agency or studio

- Story: point of view → selected work → services → process/team → fit-based inquiry.
- Media: showreel assembled from owned client work; generated abstract transitions may support it but must not masquerade as case studies.
- Interface: real case-study outcomes, clear scope, qualification questions, and contact path.

## Fitness or membership business

- Story: emotional commitment → training environment → programs/coaches → real results → schedule/membership.
- Media: consented footage of the actual space and members; avoid fabricated transformations or performance claims.
- Interface: current classes, location, waivers/requirements, pricing, and a working trial/signup path.
